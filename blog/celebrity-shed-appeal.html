The Celebrity Shed Appeal

14 September 2026

AppealFundraising

We're launching a new campaign: The Celebrity Shed Appeal. Our goal is to secure a 14x8 foot shed to keep our rabbits safe, warm and sheltered from the elements this winter.

To help us get there, we've just joined X (formerly Twitter) specifically to put this appeal directly in front of celebrities and their followings, in the hope that someone with a big platform can help us reach our goal a lot faster than we could alone.

A proper, secure shed makes a real difference for the rabbits in our care over the colder months — somewhere insulated and dry to shelter in, away from wind, rain and frost. Every donation towards this appeal goes directly towards making that happen.

Here's how you can help:

  • Follow us on X and share our appeal — https://x.com/TheLuckyLop
  • Tag any celebrities or big accounts you think might be willing to help spread the word
  • Donate directly towards the shed
  • See all the ways you can support us

Every share brings us one step closer to giving our rabbits a warm, dry home for the winter. Thank you for helping us spread the word — and if you'd rather get in touch directly, contact us here.
