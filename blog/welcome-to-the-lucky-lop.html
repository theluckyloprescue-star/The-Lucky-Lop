Welcome to The Lucky Lop

1 September 2026

News

Hello, and welcome to The Lucky Lop! We're an independent, non-profit rabbit rescue based right here in Sheffield, South Yorkshire, and this blog is where we'll be sharing updates, rescue stories, fundraising news and the occasional bit of rabbit-care advice.

We started The Lucky Lop because rabbits are so often overlooked. They're the third most commonly abandoned pet in the UK, frequently misunderstood as "easy" starter pets, and can end up neglected or given up when their needs aren't met. We wanted somewhere local and dedicated entirely to rabbits — a place that could give them proper care, a fair assessment, and the best possible chance of finding a home that actually suits them.

Every rabbit that comes through our doors gets a health check, time to settle, and a careful match to a suitable adopter. If you'd like to follow along, keep an eye on this blog and our Facebook page for updates — and if you're able to support us with a donation, a wishlist item, or by adopting, we'd love to hear from you.

Thanks for reading, and welcome to the warren!
