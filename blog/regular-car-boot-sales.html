Regular Car Boot Sales for The Lucky Lop

13 September 2026

FundraisingNews

We're excited to announce that we're planning to start holding regular car boot sales to raise funds for the rescue.

We're hoping to make these either a weekly or fortnightly event, depending on how things go. Dates, locations and full details will be announced soon.

To make this possible, we'll be looking for donations of items to sell. This could be bric-a-brac, toys, games, books, clothes, homeware or anything else you think could find a new home.

If you're having a clear-out, please consider donating your unwanted items to us rather than throwing them away. Every item sold will help us raise money towards the care of the rabbits at The Lucky Lop. 🐰❤️

More details coming soon! In the meantime, if you have items to donate or want to find out more, get in touch with us or keep an eye on our Facebook page for updates.
