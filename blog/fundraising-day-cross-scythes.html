Fundraising Day at Cross Scythes

24 October 2026

EventsFundraising

We're delighted to announce The Lucky Lop Fundraising Day, taking place at Cross Scythes on Derbyshire Lane, Sheffield, on 24th October 2026 from 3pm until 10:30pm.

Expect an evening of live music, quality food, a raffle with some brilliant prizes, and stalls from local sellers — all in aid of the rabbits currently in our care. Entry is free, with raffle tickets available for £2.

Every penny raised on the day goes directly towards vet bills, hay, bedding and housing for the rabbits waiting for their forever homes. We'd love to see you there — bring friends, bring family, and help us give these rabbits the best possible start.

Keep an eye on our Facebook page for updates and line-up details as the date gets closer.
