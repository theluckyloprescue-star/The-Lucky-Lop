Who We Are

We're a small, independent charity working across South Yorkshire, run by volunteers who believe every rabbit deserves a safe, suitable home. Rabbits are the third most commonly abandoned pet in the UK, and often the most misunderstood — we're here to change that, one rabbit at a time.

Read our full story

What We Do

Rescue

We take in unwanted, neglected and vulnerable rabbits from across the region, no matter their background.

Rehabilitate

Every rabbit is assessed, health-checked and given time to recover before they're ready to be rehomed.

Rehome

We carefully match each rabbit with a suitable forever home, with support for adopters afterwards too.

What We're Fundraising For

Right now we're raising funds towards vet bills, hay and bedding for the rabbits currently in our care. Every donation — big or small — goes directly towards their food, housing and medical treatment.
